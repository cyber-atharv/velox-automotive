Powertrain

Hybrid V12 Architecture

The LX1 pairs a naturally aspirated 6.0-litre V12 with twin axial-flux electric motors, delivering seamless power across every rev range. Total system output reaches 1,200 bhp with zero turbo lag — pure, unfiltered response.

Aerodynamics

Active Ground Effect

Derived directly from Formula 1 ground-effect principles, the LX1 generates up to 1,800 kg of downforce at maximum speed. Active underbody flaps adjust 200 times per second to maintain perfect aerodynamic balance.
